How to play

  1. Two players take turns.
  2. On your turn, answer the question by clicking the correct number on your own board.
  3. Correct answer: +10 points plus a time bonus.
  4. Wrong answer: no penalty.
  5. Complete 3 full lines (a row or column of 5 squares) to win and get +100 bonus.
